# Dropshipping Supplier & Courier Workflow
## Operational Manual for Pakistan Ecommerce

---

## 1. End-to-End Order Fulfilment Lifecycle

```
[Customer Orders via COD / WhatsApp]
                 │
                 ▼
     [Store Owner Reviews Order]
                 │
                 ▼
 [Phone Verification Call with Customer]
    ├── Customer unreachable after 3 tries ──▶ [Order Cancelled]
    └── Customer verifies address & items
                 │
                 ▼
[Order Status Updated to "Confirmed"]
                 │
                 ▼
[Forward Order Details to Dropship Supplier]
 (WhatsApp / Portal: Customer Name, Phone, City, Street Address)
                 │
                 ▼
[Supplier Packs Parcel with Secure Unboxing Seal]
                 │
                 ▼
[Supplier Books Courier (Trax / Leopards / Call Courier / TCS)]
                 │
                 ▼
[Admin Records Courier Tracking Number (CN) in Dashboard]
 [Status Transitions to "Dispatched"]
                 │
                 ▼
[Courier Delivers Parcel & Collects Cash in PKR]
    ├── Delivery Failed / Customer Refused ──▶ [Status: "Delivery Failed" / "Returned"]
    │                                          └── Courier deducts reverse shipping fee
    │
    └── Customer Pays Cash ──▶ [Status: "Delivered"]
                               └── Payment Status: "COD Collected"
                                           │
                                           ▼
                    [Courier Remits COD into Bank Account]
                                           │
                    [Admin Records Financial Settlement]
                                           │
                    [Admin Settles Wholesale Cost with Supplier]
                                           │
                    [Dashboard Reconciles Actual Net Profit]
```

## 2. Supplier Best Practices in Pakistan

1. **Wholesale Sourcing Hubs:**
   - **Shah Alam Market & Hall Road (Lahore):** Premier source for electronics, earbuds, chargers, and mobile accessories.
   - **Bolton Market & Jodia Bazaar (Karachi):** Premier hub for kitchenware, plastic household organizers, and imported goods.
   - **Raja Bazaar (Rawalpindi):** Regional distribution for Northern Punjab, KPK, and AJK.

2. **Supplier Dispatch Checklist:**
   - Confirm wholesale cost before dispatching.
   - Verify dead-on-arrival (DOA) replacement warranty terms (standard 5-7 days).
   - Ensure the supplier does **not** include their wholesale invoice inside the customer parcel.

3. **Courier Partner Comparison (Pakistan):**
   - **Trax Logistics:** Excellent online portal, fast API integration, dependable COD remittance cycle (2 times a week).
   - **Leopards Courier:** Comprehensive nationwide coverage into smaller regional towns and tehsils.
   - **Call Courier:** Competitive flat rates for 1kg shipments.
   - **TCS Express:** High brand recognition and priority handling for major metros.
